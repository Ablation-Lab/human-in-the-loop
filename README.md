# Human in the Loop

Research on keeping a human in the loop between AI agents: what it buys, what it costs, and how the human's role should change.

**Core question:** when AI agents can talk to each other directly, what does keeping a human in the middle actually buy you?

## Status

Planning. Nothing has been tested yet. The full plan, with the hypotheses, the setups to compare and how results are measured, is in [plan.md](plan.md).
